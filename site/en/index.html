01Picture

The picture the film was made to have.

Refrata reads how each video was mastered and fits the picture to your screen, frame by frame, on the graphics card. Nothing to set up: it all comes on, tuned right.

01 / 04

True HDR on HDR screens

With Windows HDR on, HDR video shows at its real brightness, up to your screen’s peak, while the interface keeps its usual brightness. Turn HDR on or off while the video plays: the picture follows in under a second.

Drag the divider to compare.

02 / 04

HDR10+ scene by scene

HDR10, HDR10+ and HLG with the right colors. Each scene’s metadata is followed and brightness is measured every frame, so dark scenes are not dimmed.

03 / 04

Smooth motion

24 or 30 fps video plays at 60, 120 or your monitor’s own refresh rate, with in-between frames computed on the GPU. No resolution limit.

04 / 04

NVIDIA RTX Video

On RTX cards, Super Resolution makes low-resolution video sharp at full screen, and RTX Video HDR turns ordinary video into HDR. Each option tells you whether it is working on the video and, if not, why.

Native up to 8K

4K, 8K and cinema 8K at their original resolution, with decoding and color conversion done by the graphics card.

Light and detail boost

Brings ordinary video to life: more open shadows and highlights, and sharpening that adapts to the picture without halos.

02Video

The film fills the screen, and the controls show only when you move the mouse.

Stop moving the mouse and everything fades, leaving just the movie. Move it and the frosted-glass controls return without covering the picture. The timeline shows where you’ll land before you click.

  • Switch audio and subtitles in one click, or drag an .srt file onto the window
  • Adjust brightness and contrast, rotate, zoom or force the aspect ratio while you watch
  • Loop a section with L and keep the window always on top with T
  • Up to 8K at native resolution, chapters on the timeline and file details with I
  • Speed from 0.25× to 4×, frame-by-frame stepping forwards and backwards, and snapshots

On Windows

Three shortcuts that change how you watch.

Press Space.
See it before you open it.

In File Explorer or on the desktop, select a video or a song and press Space. A floating preview appears instantly; press Space again to close it. The arrow keys flip through the rest of the folder, like leafing through an album.

  • Double-click opens straight in the full player, in under half a second with Refrata open
  • Want the big screen? Enter takes it to the full app, at the same second
  • Keep Refrata in the tray and starting with Windows, and everything opens with no wait

Picture-in-picture to watch while you work.

Press Ctrl+P or the button at the top of the player. Refrata steps aside and the video carries on, from the same second, in a small window that always stays on top.

  • Drag it to any corner and resize it from the edge
  • Pause, back and forward 10 s and the progress bar right in the small window
  • Double-click or Esc takes you back to the full app, at the same point in the video

YouTube links in the player, and the video on your TV with Chromecast.

On the Streams page, paste a YouTube, Vimeo or Twitch link, or an HLS, RTSP or RTMP address. Refrata finds the best quality and shows what it’s doing while it opens.

  • Over 20 sites: YouTube, Vimeo, Twitch, SoundCloud, Dailymotion, TikTok and more
  • Chromecast on the same Wi-Fi network, with the PC as the remote
  • OpenSubtitles subtitles right in the player, found from the file itself

03Tools

Subtitles that write themselves, and the tools heavy watchers ask for.

AI

AI subtitles

Refrata listens to the video and writes the subtitles with Whisper, on your graphics card, in short lines timed to the speaker, and can translate them into English. Nothing leaves the computer.

Two subtitles at once

One at the top, one at the bottom: the original and the translation together. Reads SRT, WebVTT and ASS.

ShiftE

One frame back

Besides stepping forward with E, go back one frame with Shift+E to find the exact moment.

B

Bookmarks in the video

Press B to mark a scene. The marks stay on each file’s timeline, and one click takes you back to them.

Skip intros and credits

Mark once where the intro ends and where the credits start. It applies to every episode in the folder.

AB

Clip to MP4 or GIF

Save the A-B section as a video, with no quality lost and HDR kept, or as an animated GIF to send to someone.

04Library

Your videos and music, organized without lifting a finger.

The first time it opens, Refrata reads your Videos and Music folders and builds the library with thumbnails, lengths and what you were watching. Nothing is copied or sent anywhere: it just reads the files where they already are.

Music mode

A complete music player, inside the same app.

One click plays, another pauses. The queue becomes a carousel of covers, the background takes on the album’s colors and progress runs around the play button.

  • Library with artists, albums, covers and search
  • Queue carousel with 3D covers
  • A mini player that follows you through the app

05Features

Everything a complete player needs, at hand and out of submenus.

  1. Opens almost anything

    MKV, MP4, AVI, HEVC, FLAC, Opus and HLS or RTSP streams, with the VLC engine built in. No codec packs to install.

  2. 10-band equalizer

    18 presets, a preamp, volume up to 150% and a leveller for quiet dialogue and loud explosions. Pick where the sound plays, too: speakers, headphones or TV.

  3. Subtitles right on time

    Find subtitles on OpenSubtitles without leaving the player, drag an .srt onto the window, set size and color and fix the delay when it drifts.

  4. Queue and playlists

    Play next, shuffle and repeat. Opens M3U, PLS and XSPF playlists, and loops an A-B section with one key.

  5. Sound straight to the receiver

    Dolby and DTS, and also TrueHD, DTS-HD and Atmos, go to the receiver or soundbar exactly as they are in the file.

  6. Chapters

    Marks on the timeline with each chapter's name, a list to jump straight to one, and the Shift+N and Shift+P shortcuts.

  7. Picks up where you left off

    Movies, podcasts and audiobooks return to where you stopped. Short songs start from the top, as they should.

  8. Keyboard media keys

    Play, pause and skip from the keyboard, even with Refrata minimized. It shows up in the Windows media flyout with title and cover.

  9. Shortcuts your way

    Every keyboard shortcut can be changed in Settings: click the key and press the new one. One button puts everything back.

  10. In 14 languages

    English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Hindi and seven more. It follows your Windows language, and you can switch it in Settings.

Updates

Updates through the app itself, checked before they install.

  1. 1

    A quiet heads-up

    When it opens, Refrata checks for a new version and lets you know. Nothing downloads until you click.

  2. 2

    Verified download

    Under Update, it downloads the installer and checks its SHA-256 against the one published with the release. If they don’t match, it won’t install.

  3. 3

    Back, already updated

    The install runs in the background and the app reopens on the new version, with your library and settings just as they were.

An engine that stays current

Every week the project checks whether VideoLAN has released a new VLC and whether yt-dlp, which opens links from sites, has a new version. Every engine update goes through more than a hundred automated test scenarios before it reaches you. And yt-dlp also updates itself inside the app when a site changes and a link stops opening.

If Windows gets suspicious

Refrata isn’t digitally signed yet; signing is granted to projects with a longer track record. That’s why your browser and Windows may warn that the file is rarely downloaded.

  1. In your browser, choose Keep.
  2. On the blue SmartScreen dialog, click More info, then Run anyway.

Want to check the file? Each version’s SHA-256 is published with the release.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need VLC installed?

No. The VLC engine ships inside Refrata. If you do have VLC installed, the two get along fine, each with its own settings.

Is it really free? What’s the catch?

None. Refrata is free software under the GPL 3.0 license: no ads, no account, no paid tier and no data collection. All the code is on GitHub for anyone to check.

Why does Windows warn that the file might be dangerous?

Because the installer isn’t digitally signed yet, and Windows is wary of any new program without a signature. The warning doesn’t mean there’s a virus. To check, compare the file’s SHA-256 with the one published with the release, or build the installer yourself from the code.

What do I need for true HDR?

An HDR screen with Use HDR turned on in the Windows display settings. Refrata notices on its own and shows HDR video at its real brightness, even if you turn HDR on with the app already open. On an ordinary screen, the same video is converted to SDR with the right colors.

How do AI subtitles work?

In Tracks › More subtitles, turn on AI subtitles. The first time, Refrata downloads Whisper’s speech model (82 MB, or 574 MB on cards with 3 GB of memory or more; 539 MB for translating) and checks the file. From then on, the sound is transcribed on your graphics card, offline, up to two and a half minutes ahead of what you are watching.

Do I need an NVIDIA card?

No. HDR, smooth motion and AI subtitles work with cards from any brand. Only RTX Video Super Resolution and RTX Video HDR need an NVIDIA RTX card, with the matching option also turned on in the NVIDIA App.

Does what is new in 2.0 work on Linux?

Almost all of it. True HDR output, RTX Video and AI subtitles are Windows-only for now. Smooth motion, HDR converted for ordinary screens, the second subtitle and the tools work on both.

Can I watch YouTube videos?

Yes. Copy the video’s link, open the Streams page (Ctrl+L), paste it and press Play. It also works with Vimeo, Twitch, SoundCloud, Dailymotion and other sites. Refrata picks the best quality available, and some videos take a few seconds to open.

Does it work with Chromecast?

Yes. With the PC and the Chromecast on the same Wi-Fi network, click the cast button at the top of the player and pick the TV. Pause, skip and volume stay in Refrata, which becomes the remote.

Does the VLC engine inside Refrata get out of date?

No. The project checks every week whether VideoLAN has published a new VLC. When one comes out, it is tested and goes into a Refrata update, which arrives through the app itself.

How do I make Refrata my default player?

In Settings › Quick open, press Make default: Refrata registers itself for video, music and playlist formats (the portable version too) and opens Windows’ Default apps page, where you pick Refrata.

What’s the difference between the installer and the portable version?

The installer creates shortcuts, registers file formats and lets you update from inside the app. The portable version just unzips and runs, handy for trying it without installing anything; to have it in Open with, use Make default in Settings.

What languages does the app speak?

Fourteen: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Indonesian, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. It follows your Windows language, and you can switch in Settings.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Open a report on GitHub Issues or write to lucslabs.studios@gmail.com. Tell us what you did, what you expected and what happened; if you can, add a screenshot and the version, shown in Settings.

How do I uninstall it?

In Windows Settings, go to Apps, find Refrata and click Uninstall. That also removes the start-with-Windows option if it was on.

Transparency

Open source, with no data collected.

Open source

All the code is at github.com/LucsLabsStudios/refrata, under the GPL 3.0. Official installers come from a public, automated build on GitHub Actions, from that same code. You are free to download, build and fork it. The official repository does not accept pull requests: suggestions and bugs come in through Issues, and the maintainer makes the changes.

Privacy

Refrata collects no data. On its own, it only checks GitHub’s public releases page for a new version, and PyPI when you open links from sites, to keep yt-dlp current. You can turn both off in Settings.

Code signing policy

Releases aren’t digitally signed yet. Every published file comes with its SHA-256, which the in-app updater also checks before installing. Once there is a signature, it will only be applied to files produced by the public build; no binary assembled outside it will be signed.
